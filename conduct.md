# Citizen Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

A primary goal of **Rose X Music** is to provide a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all users, contributors, and community members. We are committed to maintaining a respectful atmosphere regardless of gender, nationality, religion, ethnicity, or background.

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for everyone participating in the Rose X Music community.

Rose X Music is an open-source Telegram Music Bot project maintained by the community. We encourage respectful collaboration, innovation, and responsible development.
